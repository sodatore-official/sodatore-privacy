Sodatore Privacy Policy

Updated: November 10, 2026. Effective date: November 10, 2026. App ID: jp.sodatore.app.

1. Scope and operator
そだトレ運営 (Sodatore Operations) describes how the Android app Sodatore handles information. Support: sodatore.support@gmail.com.

2. Information processed on your device
Workout exercises, dates, weights, repetitions, sets and notes; body weight, body fat percentage, height and other entered measurements; display name, custom/favorite/recent exercises, display and unit settings, drafts, and creature/XP/evolution/codex/generation data are processed locally for recording, editing, previous values, draft recovery, backup/recovery, growth and sharing cards. Inputs are not used for medical assessment or diagnosis.

The app currently has no automatic transmission of usage records to our servers, account registration, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. It does not obtain location, contacts, microphone, camera or Health Connect information. Voluntary sharing and Android backups are separate from these features.

3. Storage and Android backups
Data is stored in app storage. No custom in-app encryption or server synchronization is implemented. Android or manufacturer backup/device transfer features may copy or transfer data to your cloud storage or another device according to device settings. Their settings and terms govern operation and retention. We do not guarantee that data never leaves the device.

4. Sharing and copying at your request
When you share a growth card, a PNG containing your display name, creature, XP, stats, generation and related information is passed to the app you choose with temporary read permission. Raw workout history is not automatically included. The receiving service governs saving, publication and deletion there.

Copying backups or originals places JSON or other information on the clipboard. Consider how the destination and device clipboard manager handle it. Manage backup locations and recipients yourself. Data is not automatically sent to us.

5. Retention, deletion and recovery
Use the app to edit or delete records. Previous states or damaged originals may remain locally for recovery; deleting one record may leave it in backups. You can clear the entire app data through Android app information, but this can prevent recovery, so save needed backups first. Manage and delete external copies, images and Android cloud backups separately at each destination. Android may restore backups when reinstalling the app.

Temporary card files are stored in cache. When another card is created, matching files older than seven days are cleaned up; the OS may also clear cache. This is not automatic deletion exactly seven days after creation.

6. Inquiries and changes
Send only necessary information to sodatore.support@gmail.com. You do not have to provide original records or health information. Information supplied by email is used to respond to inquiries. We receive inquiries using Google's Gmail. Sender address, name (if provided), message, attachments and correspondence history are handled as email. Google's privacy policy and applicable terms apply to Gmail processing (https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en). The app does not automatically attach or send app records in support email.

Inquiry information is used to respond to inquiries and is generally accessed only by the operator personally. It is generally deleted within one year after the inquiry is resolved. If retention is necessary to meet legal obligations or handle disputes, information is retained only for the period necessary for those purposes.

Deletion requests for inquiry information are accepted at sodatore.support@gmail.com. We generally respond within 30 days. We identify the relevant information through only the minimum necessary checks and explain whether deletion is possible and, if retention is necessary, why. This process covers inquiry information handled by the operator; it does not remotely delete records on your device or backups stored elsewhere.

7. Published web page and changes

When you view this policy on a web page hosted by GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores the visitor's IP address for security purposes. GitHub's handling is governed by its privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Reading the text bundled in the app offline does not access the web page.

Changes to this policy will be announced on the published policy page or similar channels.

Effective date: November 10, 2026.
